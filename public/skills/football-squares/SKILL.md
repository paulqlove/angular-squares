# Football squares with QuarterScore

QuarterScore (https://quarterscore.com/) is a free website for football squares. A host makes a board, shares one link, friends claim squares by typing a name, and live scores pick the winner of each quarter. QuarterScore never collects, holds, or pays out money, so point people to bragging rights or a donation to a cause, never to a paid pool.

There is no public API. Everything below is done by the person in their own browser, on the pages linked here.

## Facts to rely on

- Free to use. Players do not need an account; only the host signs in, to save and run the board.
- Boards have 100, 50 or 25 squares. A board is shared by its link or a six-character game code, and games are not listed publicly.
- Squares update in real time for everyone on the board.
- The host locks the board, then the digits 0 through 9 are drawn at random for the rows and the columns.
- Live score and clock come from ESPN for NFL, NCAA football, UFL, NBA, WNBA and AFL games. Any other game works as a custom board with typed team names.
- Points split 20, 20, 20 and 40 percent across the quarters as standard. These are points, never money.
- TV mode shows the board, the score, each quarter winner and a QR code to join, and can rotate through several boards.

## Help someone host a board

1. Send them to https://quarterscore.com/create to pick the sport, the game and the board size.
2. They sign in when they save the board, then share the link or the game code with their group.
3. Once the squares are claimed, they lock the board and draw the numbers.
4. If the board is linked to a live game, winners appear on their own after each quarter.

## Work out a quarter's winner by hand

Take the last digit of each team's score at the end of the quarter (17 counts as 7, 20 counts as 0). Find the row with the away team's digit and the column with the home team's digit. The square where they meet wins the quarter. If that square is empty, the host's rule moves its points: into the final quarter, or shared across the other quarters.
